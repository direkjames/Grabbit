      How to use Grabbit

      Quick start

      1. Paste a link. Copy a video link from your browser, then click Paste (or press Ctrl+V in the box). A preview appears so you know it's the right video.
      2. Pick a format. MP4 for video, MP3 for audio, WAV for lossless audio.
      3. Choose the quality. Higher is bigger and sharper. Options the video doesn't have are greyed out.
      4. Click Grab it. Watch the progress on the right under Downloads. When it says Done, click Show file.

      Which format should I pick?

      MP4Video with sound. Best for editing, reference clips and watching offline.
      MP3Audio only, smaller files. Good for music and voice. 320 kbps is the best quality.
      WAVAudio only, uncompressed. Big files, but ideal for sound editing and sound effects.

      Editor-friendly (H.264)

      Leave this on if you'll edit the video in Premiere, CapCut, Vegas or similar. It picks a video type those programs open without trouble. The catch: YouTube only offers H.264 up to 1080p. Turn it off if you want 1440p or 4K.

      Only grab a part

      Tick Only grab a part to download just a short section, like a sound effect.

      • Type the times as 0:30 or 1:02:15 (hours:minutes:seconds).
      • From is where it starts and To is where it stops.
      • Not available for playlists.

      Playlists

      If your link belongs to a playlist, a checkbox appears. Tick it to grab the whole playlist. Everything is saved in its own folder. Private or deleted videos are skipped automatically.

      Where do my files go?

      By default they go to Downloads\Grabbit. Click Change to pick another folder, or Open to jump straight to it. Past downloads are listed under the History tab.

      Good to know

      • You can start several downloads at once. Use Cancel to stop one.
      • Live streams can't be grabbed until they finish.
      • Private, age-restricted or members-only videos may not work.
      • Only grab content you have the right to use: your own uploads, royalty-free or Creative Commons material, or what your local fair-use rules allow.

      Something not working?

      • "Video unavailable" but it plays in your browser: click Update yt-dlp at the bottom of the window, wait for it to finish, then try again. YouTube changes often and this fixes most problems.
      • "That doesn't look like a link": make sure you copied the whole address, starting with https://.
      • Stuck on "Getting ready…": Grabbit is checking for updates. Make sure you're online and give it a moment.
      • Windows says "protected your PC" on first launch: click More info, then Run anyway. This only happens once.

      Tip: press F1 any time to open this guide, and Esc to close it.
